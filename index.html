About me

Marketer, analyst, founder.

I started out running paid search, SEO and email for an eCommerce bakery, where every pound of ad spend had to show up in revenue. That habit of tying activity to commercial outcomes has followed me into every role since.

At IDP Education I managed international lead-generation campaigns and the reporting behind them. At HigherAd I moved fully into analytics: multi-touch attribution in SQL, churn prediction with Python and Scikit-Learn, and executive dashboards in Tableau. Data science training with 10Alytics and growth analytics for CampusHut rounded that out.

In January 2026 I founded Revynex, an AI analyst that tells SaaS founders what changed in their revenue, why, and what to do next. It brings together what I've learned about growth, measurement and the gap between data and decisions.
